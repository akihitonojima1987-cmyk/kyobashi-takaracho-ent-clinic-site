Before your visit

  • Consultations are in Japanese. If speaking Japanese is difficult for you, please come with an interpreter (a family member or friend is fine). Without an interpreter, we may not be able to see you.
  • You can also choose a consultation using a translation app. Without Japanese health insurance (e.g. travelers), the translation fee is included in the self-pay fees. With Japanese health insurance, it costs an additional 3,300 yen (tax included, not covered by insurance).
  • Appointments are required. Our online booking system and telephone line are in Japanese only, so we recommend asking someone who reads Japanese to help you book.
  • Patients without Japanese health insurance pay the full cost themselves (from 16,500 yen, tax included).

About our clinic

Board-certified ENT specialist

An otolaryngologist certified by the Japan Society of Otorhinolaryngology–Head and Neck Surgery sees each patient carefully.

1 minute from Takaracho Station

1 min from Takaracho Station (Toei Asakusa Line), Exit A6 / A7, and 3 min from Kyobashi Station (Ginza Line). Easy to visit on your way from work.

B-spot therapy (EAT)

Treatment for chronic epipharyngitis, postnasal drip and throat discomfort. Learn more →

From children to older adults

We also see children. Elementary and junior high school students must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. High school students and older can come on their own.

What we treat

Ears

Ear infections (otitis media / externa), blocked or painful ears, hearing loss, earwax removal.

Nose

Stuffy or runny nose, hay fever and allergic rhinitis, sinusitis, nosebleeds.

Throat

Sore throat or throat discomfort, tonsillitis, hoarseness, lasting cough or phlegm.

Dizziness

Spinning or unsteady dizziness. We test for and treat dizziness caused by the ear.

Snoring and sleep apnea

If you are worried about snoring or daytime sleepiness, we discuss tests and treatment after an examination.

Children

Ear infections, runny noses and allergies in children. We try not to frighten them.

Sublingual immunotherapy

For allergic rhinitis caused by Japanese cedar pollen or house dust mites. The body gradually gets used to the allergen, aiming for long-term relief.

B-spot therapy (EAT)

Medicine is applied directly to the epipharynx for chronic throat discomfort, postnasal drip, headaches and fatigue. Learn more →
